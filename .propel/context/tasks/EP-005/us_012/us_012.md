# US-012: Execute Judge-Ready Validation and Demo Flow

## Epic Link
- Epic ID: EP-005
- Epic Name: Demo Readiness and Validation

## User Story
As a product owner,
I want a repeatable end-to-end demo script with measurable validation checkpoints,
so that the team can present a reliable and compelling story on demo day.

## Business Value
- Converts implemented capability into a strong judging outcome.
- Reduces demo failure risk under time pressure.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Demo covers medication guidance, condition explanation, and one follow-up turn chain.
2. Demo includes one emergency escalation scenario.
3. Demo includes one off-scope or medication-boundary scenario.
4. Personalization checks verify referenced patient facts are accurate.
5. Validation report captures pass or fail status for SM-001 to SM-005 and QG-001 to QG-004.

# US-009: Enforce Emergency Escalation Guardrails

## Epic Link
- Epic ID: EP-003
- Epic Name: Clinical Guardrails and Escalation

## User Story
As a safety officer,
I want emergency symptom triggers to always override normal response generation,
so that high-risk patients are immediately directed to emergency care.

## Business Value
- Protects patient safety in the highest-risk interaction path.
- Satisfies top judging criterion for guardrail correctness.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Emergency patterns such as chest pain and breathing difficulty are detected deterministically.
2. Emergency detection bypasses normal LLM response generation.
3. Escalation output is explicit and unambiguous.
4. Emergency response does not include minimization language.
5. Guardrail activation is logged with rule ID and reason.

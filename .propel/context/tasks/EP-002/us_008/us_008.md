# US-008: Support Care Plan, Appointment, Lifestyle, and Multi-Turn Follow-Up

## Epic Link
- Epic ID: EP-002
- Epic Name: Conversational Guidance Core

## User Story
As a patient user,
I want follow-up conversation continuity across care-plan and lifestyle questions,
so that I can ask natural multi-step questions without repeating context.

## Business Value
- Increases trust by making the assistant feel coherent and context-aware.
- Supports care plan adherence and pre-visit readiness.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Appointment and care plan questions use active profile schedule and task data.
2. Lifestyle guidance questions reference profile context where relevant.
3. Follow-up questions resolve references from prior turns for at least 5 to 10 exchanges.
4. Conversation does not contradict previously provided answers in same session.
5. Timestamps and turn separation are visible in UI transcript.
